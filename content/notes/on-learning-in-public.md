Learning in public can easily become a performance: frequent updates, polished certainty, and an audience quietly turning into the measure of whether the learning matters. That is not what I want this notebook to become.

My reason for keeping it public is simpler. When I know that another person might read an explanation, I write with greater care. I notice where I have hidden uncertainty behind vague language, where two ideas do not quite connect, and where I have repeated a definition without understanding it. The audience may be hypothetical; the discipline it creates is real.

There is good evidence for this intuition. Research on self-explanation found that students who explained material to themselves while learning produced more inferences and developed stronger understanding than students who did not [1]. Writing here is my version of that process. The aim is not to make every difficult idea sound easy. It is to discover precisely where my own explanation stops working.

## What goes here

Not everything I think or read or work on. Just the things that need somewhere to sit while I think about them more. Sometimes a note matures into an essay. Sometimes it just stays a note. Sometimes I write it and realise partway through that I had the question wrong.

The imperfection is intentional. A notebook containing only finished thoughts is an archive, and I need somewhere for ideas to remain unfinished while they earn their shape.

## What does not go here

I try not to put things here that are not real. No exaggerated confidence in things I am still uncertain about. No performance of expertise I do not have. If I do not know something, I say so. If a note is rough, I mark it as rough.

The status fields in the frontmatter are part of this. "Draft" means it is not ready. "Evergreen" means I think it is worth keeping. "Active question" means I am still working on it.

## Curio Synapse is different

The notes here are mine. They are part of how I think, not necessarily how I would teach. Curio Synapse is where I take ideas that are ready to help someone else understand something and develop them into something more structured.

Some notes here will become Curio Synapse lessons. Most will not. That is fine.
